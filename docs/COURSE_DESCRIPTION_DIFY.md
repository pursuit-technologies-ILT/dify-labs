## Title
**No-Code Agentic AI: Building Business Automation with Dify**

## Overview
Build no-code agentic AI workflows for business automation and collaboration through **Dify**, applied throughout to health insurance scenarios such as member service, claims, and prior authorization. Agentic AI foundations cover what agentic AI is, how it differs from other AI techniques, and the collaboration and automation use cases that show up in a health plan. The components of an AI agent cover foundation models, model selection, working and episodic memory, and the OODA decision pattern. Tool use and MCP servers cover observation versus action tools, direct and indirect tool-use methods, and integrating MCP servers (or equivalent HTTP/OpenAPI tools) for shared capabilities. Building agentic applications, designing collaborative agents, and the ethics and security of agentic AI cover task decomposition, multi-agent systems, automation versus human collaboration, fairness, transparency, accountability, security threats, and excessive-agency risks. Hands-on labs on **Dify** (with an optional **Open WebUI** trainee portal) produce working health insurance agents, human-in-the-loop workflows, and an excessive-agency risk audit. The course is designed for business users learning to apply agentic AI at a health insurance organization.

**Duration:** 2 Days

## Skills Gained
By the end of this course, participants will be able to:
- Define agentic AI as distinct from other AI techniques
- Describe the components of an AI agent (model, memory, tools, OODA loop)
- Configure best practices for designing and implementing AI agents
- Build basic AI agents for automation and human collaboration in **Dify**
- Identify key ethical and security risks of agentic AI

## Who Can Benefit
- Business Analysts & Process Owners
- Operations & Support Teams
- Team Leads & Managers Automating Workflows
- Anyone New to Agentic AI

## Prerequisites
- Familiarity with basic Generative AI concepts such as prompting

## Organizational Objectives
- Reduce time-to-first-agent for business teams through no-code **Dify Apps**
- Build a shared understanding of agentic AI capabilities and limits across non-engineering teams
- Lower governance risk through trained recognition of excessive-agency and ethical issues, including PHI exposure
- Standardize human-in-the-loop patterns for agent-driven automation

## Software / Lab access
- Modern web browser
- Access to the training lab:
  - **Builders:** Dify Studio
  - **Trainees:** published Dify App UI and/or Open WebUI
- Model access via OpenRouter (lab) or the organization’s approved LLM gateway
